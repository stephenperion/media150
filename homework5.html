As I was choosing images for this assignment, there were a bunch of images that I could’ve chosen. Though, the photo I chose captured the overall ambiance that I would personally have liked for my own home — a home with peace, serenity, and an aesthetic layout. I then continued to use Adobe Illustrator to fully capture the overall ambiance that the original photo had. Using a triad color scheme, and its shades, I was able to create an illustrated image that captured the overall presence of the original photo. The result of my illustration reminded me of my childhood shows, such as the Fairly OddParents, Ben10, and Max & Ruby, in which I had fond memories of.
