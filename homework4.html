Homework 4: Photomontage

The concept I wanted to pursue with my photomontage is the dire status of the axolotl. As of current, the status of the axolotls is endangered, and this was the overall message I wanted to convey through Photomontage.

Through the techniques I learned in Linked-in Learning about Photoshop, I was able to layer images of the ocean, axolotls, and the hands that I found on the internet. Once I acquired all the images that I wanted to have in my Photomontage I was able to change the opacity to better convey and simplify the design of my Photomontage to better convey my intended message.
