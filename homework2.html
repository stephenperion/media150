Yellow Cab

Going through the Hunter College sky bridge I was randomly talking and taking photos with my friend from the same MEDPL 150 class. One of the topics we were discussing about was how to change the color balance on the camera, while adjusting the settings, I thought that it would be cool if I took a photo of something contrasting the current color balance of the camera.

Taking photos randomly, I was able to quickly take a photo of this yellow cab, in which I was able to further emphasize with cropping the image in the middle of the Rule of Thirds and highlighting its yellow more with tonal corrections in Photoshop

The Sun Block


This photo was captured in my time walking around Hunter with the cameras given by my MEDPL 150 Lab instructor. Fixing some of the settings with the camera I was able to make a sharp capture of the Thomas Hunter building with the sun directly hitting it. Through small edits with crops and contrast I was able to emphasize the sun’s light onto the building.
